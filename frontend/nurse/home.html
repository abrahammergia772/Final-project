About the hospital

Otona Campus, since 1920.

It began as a small clinic of the Sudan Interior Mission in 1920. It served as a primary hospital for 50 years, then as a general hospital for 30 years. In 2009 it joined Wolaita Sodo University as a teaching and referral hospital.

The campus is in the highland of Otona, close to Damota Mountain. The college lists about 450 academic staff and more than 1,250 regular undergraduate students.

Otona Campus, Wolaita Sodo, Ethiopia. P.O. Box 138.
